# SyncStock conversion operating kit

Updated October 30, 2026 (America/Chicago). Prepared for the owner's portfolio-wide conversion request. This is a documentation-only change: it does not redesign, deploy, change pricing, or restart held development.

## Positioning and first qualification

Working message: **Know which paid Shopify orders reached QuickBooks—and which need attention.**

Ask whether the merchant's accounting workflow actually requires individual QuickBooks Online Sales Receipts before proposing SyncStock. Prefer one store, one QBO company and one currency inside the verified supported workflow. Qualify the current connector, paid-order volume, product mapping, manual checking time, unresolved exceptions and the person reviewing bookkeeping.

Do not market payout reconciliation, inventory synchronization, automated refund accounting or complete bookkeeping. Existing plan prices are Solo $8/month (200 orders), Scale $29/month (1,000), Empire $49/month (unlimited), with the first 20 synced orders free. New merchant billing uses Shopify; never replace it with the legacy Stripe flow.

## Short outreach draft

Subject: Shopify receipts in QuickBooks

Hi [verified first name],

Do your Shopify clients need individual sales receipts in QuickBooks, or is payout-level bookkeeping the better fit?

SyncStock focuses on paid-order receipts, product mapping and visible exceptions. I can show the narrow sandbox workflow so you can assess whether it solves a real checking problem.

Would a short walkthrough be useful?

[Sender identity and normal business/opt-out footer]

This is an unsent template. Use only a verified recipient and source-grounded opening, after checking existing contact history and current authorization. Current App Store/review state must be checked before using installation or availability language.

## Follow-up assets

After at least four business days without reply, if covered by authorization:
"One fit question before I close this out: are missing receipts or product mappings something you currently check manually? If your present connector already handles that reliably, SyncStock may not add value."

After another five business days:
"I'll close this for now. If individual Shopify receipts become a bookkeeping headache, I can share the supported workflow and its current limitations."

No automatic follow-up is established by this file. Stop on refusal, opt-out, bounce, active reply, unsupported workflow or lack of permission. Use dedicated business profiles, not personal TikTok/Facebook accounts.

## Proof and release gate

The existing README and docs/LAUNCH.md record a narrow $10 development-store/QBO sandbox result. That is not customer evidence or current broad production acceptance. Keep the existing three-merchant protocol as the detailed acceptance plan. Do not claim time saved until measured, App Store approval until verified, or accounting correctness from matching totals alone.

A useful proof asset is a clearly labeled sandbox walkthrough showing an eligible order, one matching receipt, an exception and its recovery. Reuse completed screenshots and evidence rather than recreating them without a documented need.

## Measurement contract

Use one unique lead ID and a source URL/permission reference, never private customer data in this public repository.

- qualified discussion: individual-receipt need plus supported workflow confirmed
- walkthrough completed: observed completion, separate from a link sent
- pilot accepted: scope and participation explicitly accepted
- activation: supported integration and independently reviewed expected receipt verified
- paid: authoritative Shopify subscription/payment evidence, not an email or checkout visit
- retained: active paid customer at a stated later date; no inferred lifetime value

Record raw compatible counts before rates. Track acquisition minutes, onboarding/support minutes, actual fees, refunds and hosting allocations. Unknown costs remain unknown. Net cash contribution and time-adjusted contribution are separate; use an owner-approved hourly value only for the latter.

## First controlled test

Hypothesis: a receipt-workflow fit question earns more relevant discussions than an all-purpose sync pitch.
Change: test the short message against the next explicitly authorized qualified cohort.
Primary outcome: qualified discussions per contacted eligible company.
Downstream outcomes: accepted pilots, verified activations and paid retention.
Guardrail: no unsupported accounting promise, no launch-state exaggeration, no live-books cutover without its separate acceptance.
Review: after the authorized sequence has completed; a small sample is directional, not a statistically established winner.
